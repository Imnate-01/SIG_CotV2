## A Real Quote, Step by Step

Here's what creating a US Standard Rate quote looks like inside the system.

### Step 1 — Fill in the Customer Info

You enter the **customer name**, **city/state**, **scope of visit**, and **equipment to be serviced**. That's it. No formatting, no headers to set up.

```
Customer:          Lyons Magnus, Inc.
City / State:      Fresno, CA
Scope of Visit:    SF40 line install and commissioning
Equipment:         CFA 405-32 FP
Payment Terms:     Net 30 Days
Quoted By:         [Your Name]
```

---

### Step 2 — Add Travel Legs

Click **+ Add Leg** for each travel segment. The system calculates the cost instantly using the current 2026 standard rates.

| Date | From | To | Reg Hrs | Sat Hrs | Sun Hrs |

| 5/11/2026 | Monterrey, MX | Fresno, CA | 8 | 0 | 0 |

**Travel Subtotal → calculated automatically ✓**

> No manual formulas. No cell references. No broken links.

## Common Questions & Real Answers

**"I'm worried I'll make a mistake in the system."**
> The system validates inputs and auto-calculates everything. The only thing you type is what you already know: customer info, dates, and hours. There are no formulas to break.

**"What if I need to go back and change a quote?"**
> All saved quotes are accessible from the dashboard. You can view, edit, or delete them at any time.

**"What if the rates change next year?"**
> Rates are updated system-wide. Every new quote automatically uses the current rates. Old quotes remain archived with the rates they were created with.

**"What if I don't have internet access on-site?"**
> You can prepare and save the quote before your trip. The PDF can be downloaded and sent from your email like any attachment.

**"Is this replacing our other tools too?"**
> No. SIG System is specifically for quotations. It works alongside everything else you already use.

**"What if I need a quote that doesn't fit the standard format?"**
> The Additional Amounts section gives you flexibility: add preparation fees, expenses, calibration charges, or apply a sales accommodation (discount) — all accounted for in the grand total.

**"Who can see my quotes?"**
> Your team. That's the point. If you're out of office, someone else can pull up your quote and help the customer without starting from scratch.

## Getting Started

1. **Log in** at the SIG System URL provided by your manager

3. Click **"New US Quote"**
4. Fill in the form — it takes under 10 minutes
5. Click **"Preview"** to see the PDF
6. Click **"Save"** — your quote is stored and numbered automatically
