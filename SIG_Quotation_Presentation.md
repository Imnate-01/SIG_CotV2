## 🔄 The New Philosophy: How Quotations Work

In a traditional office setup, a quotation is a **static file** (such as an Excel or Word sheet) sitting on a hard drive. If a formula breaks, a price updates, or a colleague goes on sick leave, the quote stalls.

The **SIG System** introduces a modern workflow where a quote is a **dynamic database asset**:

```
      SPREADSHEET PHILOSOPHY                    SIG ECOSYSTEM PHILOSOPHY
  ┌─────────────────────────────┐           ┌─────────────────────────────┐
  │         Static File         │           │    Central Database Asset   │
  │ • Locked on local drive     │           │ • Accessible by entire team │
  │ • Manual formula security   │   VS.     │ • Calculated on secure API  │
  │ • Isolated from client data │           │ • Fully linked to machinery │
  │ • Easy to lose or duplicate │           │ • One-click PDF rendering   │
  └─────────────────────────────┘           └─────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Lifecycle of a Quote in the System:
1. **Drafting (Borrador):** The quote is initialized. Rates are fetched in real-time, totals are calculated automatically, and a dynamic PDF preview is rendered on-screen.
2. **Collaboration:** Because the quote lives in the cloud, any authorized team member or manager can log in, review the draft, adjust hours, or approve pricing instantly.
3. **Delivery:** The system merges the technical Field Service Report (FSR) with the quote and drafts the follow-up email in professional English.
4. **Fulfillment (Aceptada / Rechazada):** Once the client approves the quote, the Purchase Order (PO) status is updated directly on the dashboard, closing the loop with accounting and scheduling.

## 🇺🇸 The United States (US) Entity Workflow

The primary breakthrough of the SIG Quotation Engine is its **native US Entity workflow**. Instead of forcing you to use separate platforms or Excel templates, the system adapts instantly when you select **`US`** (or `CA`) as the **Issuing Entity** (`entidad`).

```
                      ┌───────────────┐
                      │ Entity Toggle │
                      └───────┬───────┘
            ┌─────────────────┼─────────────────┐
            ▼                 ▼                 ▼
        [ MX ]             [ US ]            [ CA ]
   ─────────────────  ─────────────────  ─────────────────
   • Spanish PDFs     • English PDFs     • English PDFs
   • MXN / USD        • USD Default      • USD Default
   • MX Tax Catalog   • US Rate Catalog  • CA Rate Catalog
   • Standard Fields  • Custom US Fields • Custom US Fields
```

### 1. Initialization & Automatic USD Tariff Fetching
* When the coordinator sets the entity to **`US`**, the system locks the currency to **`USD`**.
* The application automatically triggers a secure API request: `/servicios?region=US`.
* This fetches only the active US tariff catalog in dollars, making it impossible to quote wrong-country rates.

### 2. Smart Client Selection & Plant-Level Machinery Filter
* Selecting a US corporate client (e.g. *Refresco US*, *Kerry Ingredients*, or *Lyons Magnus*) auto-completes two vital addresses:
  * **Bill To (Sold-To):** The corporate billing address in the United States.
  * **Service Location (Ship-To):** The specific physical plant where the work is performed.
* **Plant-Level Filtering:** The **Machine Combobox** dynamically filters to show only the machines registered at that exact physical plant (e.g., *Refresco Plant #2* ➔ Model `CFA 406-32` / Serial `850451014`). This guarantees high precision with zero typing.

### 3. Dedicated US Service Fields & AI Improvers
The interface adapts to show fields required by US corporate procurement:
* **Scope of Visit:** Explains the technical objectives of the dispatch in English (e.g. *"Quarterly preventive maintenance on filling block & valve replacement"*).
* **Equipment to be Serviced:** Auto-populates from the selected plant machinery.
* **Payment Terms:** Standard net terms (e.g. *Net 30 days*, *Due upon receipt*).
* **AI Observation Improver:** Coordinators can type informal notes and click **"Improve AI"**. The system instantly rewrites them into professional, technical English specifications.

### 4. Service Configuration & Labor Breakdown Audit
* Add service items from the USD rate catalog.
* **Detailed Engineer Labor Logs:** For line items that require a detailed breakdown (like travel or overtime blocks), the coordinator can open an interactive sub-panel.
* Enter the **engineer's name** and their **exact hours** per service line. This provides transparent labor data that easily passes customer audits.

## 📄 The Dynamic PDF Document Engine (isUS = True)

Clicking **"Preview"** compiles a gorgeous, commercial PDF in real-time. Because `entidad` is set to US, the generator activates `isUS = true` and applies premium styling rules:

* **100% Translated Layout:** Every label is translated to English commercial standards:
  * `PROVEEDOR` ➔ **`VENDOR`** (Using SIG's US corporate headquarters address).
  * `FACTURAR A` ➔ **`BILL TO (SOLD TO)`**.
  * `LUGAR DEL SERVICIO` ➔ **`SERVICE LOCATION (SHIP TO)`**.
  * `COTIZACIÓN` ➔ **`QUOTE`**.
  * `FECHA` ➔ **`DATE`**.
* **Localized Date Format:** Dates are automatically output in American format (e.g., `May 22, 2026`).
* **Automated Signatures & Roles:** The system dynamically attaches the coordinator's digital signature (e.g., *John Santoyo*) and auto-translates their professional title (e.g., **`Service Sales Manager`** or **`Back Office Manager`**).
* **Dynamic Font Scaling:** If the quote contains numerous line items or complex labor logs, our layout engine automatically scales the typography down (from 1.0x down to 0.65x) so that the entire document **always fits perfectly on a single page**.

## 🔗 Integrated Tools for Speed & Professionalism

### 📎 1. Drag-and-Drop Browser PDF Merger
Technicians generate a Field Service Report (FSR) on-site. Coordinators generate a Quote. Under the US workflow, you can combine them instantly:
* Simply drag & drop the FSR PDF file directly into the browser's preview panel.
* The application uses `pdf-lib` to merge the generated Quote and the FSR into a single file right inside the browser.
* Clicking download delivers a unified, comprehensive document: **`Cotizacion_SIG-US-{folio}_FULL.pdf`**.

### 🧠 2. AI Client Address Extractor
When a client sends a new address or contact block in an unformatted email, click the **AI Client Extractor**. Paste the raw, dirty text, and the AI will parse and auto-complete the Sold-To and Ship-To fields for you.

### ✉️ 3. AI English Email Generator
Once the quote is saved, the system calls `/ia/generar-correo` and drafts a perfect follow-up email in English, prepopulating the contact name, quote folio, services summary, and USD total. Click **"Copy"**, paste it into Outlook, and send!

## ⚡ The 4-Step US Quoting Workflow

Getting started takes seconds. Here is how easy it is to issue a professional, merged US quote:

1. **Initialize:** Click **New Quotation** and toggle the *Entidad Emisora* to **US** or **CA**.
2. **Select Client:** Choose a client (e.g. *Refresco US*). The system instantly auto-fills the Sold-To and Ship-To addresses and loads the USD rate catalog.
3. **Configure & Audit:** Add labor lines, input hours, log engineers, and fill in the *Scope of Visit*, *Equipment*, and *Payment Terms*.
4. **Preview & Merge:** View the English PDF live in the browser. Drag and drop the technician's Field Service Report to merge the files, click **Save** to lock in your unique US folio number (e.g. `SIG-US-104`), and copy your AI-drafted English email.
